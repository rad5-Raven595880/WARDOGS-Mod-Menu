Wardogs - Mod menu


WARDOGS is a first-person shooter that combines gunplay and combined arms combat with building and destruction mechanics set in a combat sandbox for up to 100 players.

In this King of the Hill-inspired game mode, three teams battle for control of a randomly generated 2 x 2 km zone on a 256 km² map. The team with the most players in the control zone scores points. The first team to reach 100 points wins the match. The varied zone layout and wide range of available tactics ensure no two matches are the same.
